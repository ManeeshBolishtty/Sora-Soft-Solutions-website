03 / IDEAS IN DEVELOPMENT

A SORASOFT PRODUCT

Meet the idea behind
SoraTrade.

The idea began with a familiar challenge: trading records, calculations and performance reviews can be difficult to keep organised. SoraTrade is being developed to bring these tasks into a clearer workflow, helping users record trades and review their decisions in one place.

In development. Planned features and availability may change as the product takes shape. SoraTrade is intended for record-keeping and review, not profit guarantees or trading advice.
